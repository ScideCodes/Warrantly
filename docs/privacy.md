# Warrantly privacy policy

## In short

Warrantly keeps your warranties on your phone. There is no Warrantly server, no account, no ads and no tracking. The developer cannot see your data.

## Gmail

Gmail import is optional. Warrantly asks for Gmail access only when you tap Add Gmail account.

With your permission, Warrantly reads your Gmail (read-only) on this phone to find warranty documents and mails that state a warranty. Only those mails are downloaded, and only the details needed for a warranty are kept: store, product, dates, order number, contact details and attached PDFs. Mail is never sent anywhere else, and Warrantly never sends, deletes or changes mail.

Warrantly's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Backups

If you turn backups on, your data is encrypted on this phone with your backup password and stored in your own Google Drive, in a folder only Warrantly can see. Without the password nobody can read it, the developer included. Android's own app backup is switched off for Warrantly, so its database never leaves the phone unencrypted.

## Scans and documents

Scanning, reading serial numbers and reading text from receipts for search happen on the phone (Google ML Kit, on-device). Documents are stored in the app on this phone and, if backups are on, encrypted in your Drive.

## Other services

App updates are downloaded from Expo (expo.dev), which sees your IP address like any website. Reminders are scheduled on the phone. Errors are logged on the phone and only leave it if you attach them to a feedback mail.

## Your choices

Settings > Your data exports everything or deletes everything, including the Drive backups. Disconnect a Gmail account in Settings, and remove Warrantly's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is shown in the app.
